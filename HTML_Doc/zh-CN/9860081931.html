RXM39.1 产品描述

I/O 块包含由房间自动化站通过 KNX PL-Link 控制的输入和输出。它们与 PXC3.. 系列房间自动化站兼容。

RXM39.1, KNX PL-Link I/O block

  • 2 temperature inputs LG-Ni 1000
  • 4 binary inputs
  • 2 potential-free relay contacts to release fan (5 A) and electric heater (10 A)
  • 3 analog outputs (0...10 V) for the control of ECM fans, valve actuators and electric heating

订单号：S55376-C105
