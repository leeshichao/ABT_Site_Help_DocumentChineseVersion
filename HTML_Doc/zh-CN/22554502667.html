Show details of a ABT Site BACnet/SC certificate

  1. Go to Settings.
  2. Open the Certificates task.
  3. Select the BACnet/SC certificates tab.
  4. In the toolbar, click Show details.
  5. The Certificate dialog box from Windows opens.
  6. The certificate information displays.
