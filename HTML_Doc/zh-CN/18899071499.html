在项目树中手动替换 I/Os

您可以使用实际配置和测试的 I/Os. 来替换它们，而不是使用从库中添加的工厂的 I/Os

  1. Copy a new plant from the library.
  2. Modify the control program according to the requirements.
  3. Drag the actual configured and tested I/Os and drop it onto the I/Os in the list you intend to replace.
  4. Confirm with Replace.
