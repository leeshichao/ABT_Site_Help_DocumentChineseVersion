CET airflow tracking (CetAirFlTck11)

Overview

应用功能》CET room pressurization air volume flow tracking 11" (CetAirFlTck11）计算房间内供气和排气终端的流量设定值以完成加压。供应流量和抽取流量之间的差异是一个固定的配置值。选择设定点是为了在终端的最小和最大流量的限制内完成加热、冷却和通风。

AF 平衡其控制下的房间流量（通常是供应和抽气终端）和独立的排气流量（例如通风柜）。

主要特点：

  • Airflow coordination for pressurized rooms, including:
    • Single tracking pair (one supply, one extract)
    • Lab room with fume hood (one supply, one extract, and fume hood exhaust)
    • Large lab with fume hoods (multiple supply, extract and fume hoods)
  • Coordination of airflow demands for heating, cooling, and ventilation
  • Airflow monitoring including warning and alarm values (configurable)
  • Multiple segment support
    (A segment can include any of the following: supply terminal / extract terminal / supply + extract pair / independent exhaust terminal - fume hood).
  • Alarms
    • Air flow transfer alarm
  • Warnings
    • Air flow transfer warning
  • Flow monitoring values
    • Total supply
    • Total extract
    • Air flow transfer
  • Other supply/ other exhaust

CAUTION

气流污染物警报和控制

为了有效管理空气污染物，用户通常需要 BACS 在气流跟踪系统无法正常运行时发出警报。此外，用户经常需要确认气流控制和污染物控制之间的关系。

  1. Installing contractors and service technicians should work with end users to ensure alarms and operating procedures are effective.
  2. A room pressure monitor may be warranted or required.

 

Function

PRMod
Room pressurization mode (PRMod) 可设置为中性、正压或负压房间压力。根据 PRMod 的设置，以三种方式（模式）之一计算气流设定值。

  • Positive - the supply flow equals exhaust plus transfer.
    Transfer flow is outward. Room pressure is higher than surroundings.
  • Negative - the exhaust flow equals supply plus transfer.
    Transfer flow is inward. Room pressure is lower than surroundings.
  • Neutral - the supply flow equals exhaust, and the AF does not use the transfer flow (flow offset) value.
    Net transfer flow is almost zero. Direction of transfer flow is unreliable. Room pressure is the same as surroundings.

SpAirFlTrn
Transferred air volume flow setpoint (SpAirFlTrn) 是通过房间边界上的开口进入或离开加压房间的空气量的设定值。

在加压房间中，抽气流量设定值与送风流量设定值的差异为SpAirFlTrn.

示例：在负压房间中，如果供气 = 600 且 AirFlTrn = 100，则抽气流量设定值 = 700。在正压房间中，如果供气 = 600 且 AirFlTrn = 100，则抽气流量设定值 = 500。（净流向由下式表示）PRMod价值）

AirFlTrnR
Room transferred air volume flow (AirFlTrnR) 是所有气流测量的计算值。它是为监控和报警而计算的。在这个AF中，控制器的作用是驱动AirFlTrnR到设定点（SpAirFlTrn）。 （发送到段中终端的设定点信号按百分比缩放。）

监控加压室的操作员可以检查 AirFlTrnR 的值。如果它是一个正值，那么房间的压力方向是正确的；如果为负，则气流传输方向与预期方向相反。

  • When PRMod is set to Positive (or Neutral), transfer flow is defined as supply minus extract
  • When PRMod is set to Negative, transfer flow is defined as extract minus supply

AirFlSuR
Room supply air volume flow(AirFlSuR) 是送风室的总风量流量。这包括各段的供电端子和AirFlSuAdd.

AirFlExR
Room extract air volume flow (AirFlExR) is the total extract room air volume flow. This includes exhaust terminals in the segments andAirFlExAdd.

TckMod
Tracking mode (TckMod) 确定流量跟踪计算的顺序。提取轨道供应最好用于正压；供应轨道提取物最适合负压。

TckMthd
Tracking method (TckMthd) determines how tracking is accomplished, tracking method is based on setpoint or flow. If setpoint tracking is selected and the system cannot reach or maintain setpoint, flow tracking is used.

Fume hood flow
Collected from the fume hoods that are configured as members of the CetRCtl group. The AF handles changes in the fume hood flow by adjusting supply, extract, or both.

Other supply / other extract
AF 从通风柜收集动态流量数据并重新计算供应和提取设定值。该 AF 处理不在其控制范围内但属于室内空气平衡一部分的其他供气和抽气设备。其他流数据可以是静态的（配置的）或动态的（由网络上的另一个设备写入）。

Flow Rates for supply and extract
系统选择满足每个功能的所有要求的最低流量：加压、通风、加热/冷却。控制优先级按以下顺序执行：

  1. Maximum flow limits for terminals
  2. Pressurization (SpAirFlTrn) in the desired direction, not less than required level
  3. Ventilation rate for room
  4. Temperature control (heating / cooling functions)
  5. Minimum flow limits for terminals
  6. Pressurization (AirFlTrnR) in the desired direction, not greater than required level

在极少数情况下，实验室 DXR 可能会将流量设置为低于配置的最小值或在超过气流传输设定点时强制执行流量限制。有关详细信息，请参阅技术原理 > 实验室 > 下的“特殊情况”Pressurization for critical spaces.

Transferred air volume flow alarm configuration
▶ 根据客户需要设置监控房间压力。

1:Disabled
▶ 警报永远不会触发。如果客户使用其他方法来监控压力，可能是一个不错的选择。对于维护或机械故障等特殊情况，可以临时选择。
2:Alarm on high value
▶ 仅在输送量过高时发出警报，但在输送量过小或方向错误时不会发出警报。通常没有必要，因为过多的气流传输通常不会导致污染问题。在空间间加压程度复杂的场景中可能有用。在这种情况下，过多的净传输流可能会在边界之一发生逆流。这很不寻常。
3:Alarm on low value
▶ 通常是监测加压的最佳选择。当输送流量过小或方向错误时设置警报。当传输流量高于所需值时不会发出警报。
4:Alarm on high or low value
▶ 通常没有必要，因为过多的气流传输通常不会导致污染问题。在空间间加压程度复杂的场景中可能有用。
