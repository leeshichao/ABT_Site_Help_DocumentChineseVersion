加湿控制 (HumCtl11)

Overview

应用功能》Humidification control 11" (HumCtl11）协调房间部分中的一个或多个加湿设备，以将房间湿度维持在所需的水平。

主要特点：

  • Room relative humidity control
  • Humidity monitoring alarm support
  • Humidity setpoint configurable per room operating mode

 

Humidification – Issues of concern

潮湿空气中的冷凝可能会出现在意想不到的地方。湿气穿过建筑物迁移并进入外墙和建筑材料层之间。隐藏在墙壁内的凝结水可能是一场灾难。

值得关注的问题包括：

  • Water spray where it is not needed (for example, water collection inside duct)
  • Water spray when it is not needed (for example, seasonal high humidity)
  • Ineffective high limit control, duct air
  • Bends and changes in the duct; obstructions in the duct
  • Ineffective temperature control (too cold air causes condensation)

CAUTION

潮湿危害

建筑控制中的加湿会带来财产损失和不安全状况的风险。无这些问题背景或责任的人员必须遵守以下规定：

  1. DO NOT select room humidity setpoints.
  2. DO NOT select which modes enable and disable humidification.
  3. DO NOT pick the method for high limit control in the duct.
  4. DO NOT choose not to apply high limit control – send RFI if it is not in the spec.
  5. DO NOT alter dispersion tube Location or Pitch. If instructions are unclear contact the Humidifier Representative.
  6. DO NOT jumper anything in the package unit without written authorization from Humidifier Representative.
  7. DO NOT leave the Humidity system energized and untested.
  8. DO NOT energize Humidifier before the device has been commissioned.
  9. On all of these points, follow the spec. If the spec does not specify, then you must send an RFI.

Function

HumCtl11 通过图表输入引脚接收来自各段的湿度传感器读数，并将其与当前湿度设定值进行比较。 PID 湿度控制器计算需求输出，该需求输出由组经理分配到房间部分。

Operating mode effects
HumCtl11 启用 /disables 加湿功能并根据房间操作模式和工厂模式选择湿度设定点。请参阅下表以及配置部分。

Available signal

组管理员从房间部分收集“可用”信号（指示加湿设备是否可供使用）。如果没有可用的加湿设备，则禁用PID以防止积分累积。

 

Humidification request to segments
HumCtl11 传达加湿需求（PID 输出）和加湿功能的启用/disable 状态，通过组数据交换对加湿器进行分段。这两个动态值（需求和启用/disable）组合成一个请求信号，如下表所示。
