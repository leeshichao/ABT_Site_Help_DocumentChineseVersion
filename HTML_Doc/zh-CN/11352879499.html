保存诊断数据

为了在 ABT 站点中提供调试和诊断信息，必须首先读取数据并将其保存到项目中。

您可以为每台设备保存多个数据系列。每个保存的数据系列都会在相应的设备下保存一个时间戳。

要查看保存的信息，请访问Startup > Commissioning and diagnostics.
Commissioning and diagnostics

Save diagnostic data

  1. At least one device must be connected.
    Connecting to devices
  1. Open the Startup > Configure and download task.
  2. In the Discovered devices tab, select a device.
  3. Right-click and select Save diagnostic data.
  4. Select the scope of diagnostic information to be saved.
  5. Click OK.
  1. The data is now available in ABT Site.
  2. To view the saved information, go to Startup > Commissioning and diagnostics.
