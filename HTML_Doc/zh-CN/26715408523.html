导出 IEEE 802.1X 的签名证书

导出根证书（证书颁发机构的证书），以便将根证书安装到客户端

  1. Go to Settings.
  2. Open the Certificates task.
  3. Select the IEEE 802.1X tab.
  4. In the toolbar, click Export signing certificates for IEEE 802.1X.
  5. The root certificate is exported and located, for example, C:\Users\Public\Documents\Siemens\DESIGO\Exports.
