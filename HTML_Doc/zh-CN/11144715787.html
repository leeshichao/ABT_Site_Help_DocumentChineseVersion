调试和诊断

为了在 ABT 站点中提供调试和诊断信息，必须首先读取数据并将其保存到项目中。

Saving diagnostic data
Saving commissioning data

 

可以保存、分析和导出调试和诊断信息，例如以便现场支持进行进一步分析。诊断信息可包括以下内容：

  • Data point test information, such as history, tester, time stamps etc.
  • Controller device object information
  • BACnet objects carrying information on the controller and the field busses
  • Grouping information such as information about group managers and members
  • Collected trend data
  • Information from the central event logger.
