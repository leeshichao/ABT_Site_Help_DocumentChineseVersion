UP 258 个扩展属性

这些设备支持以下子系统特定的扩展：

  • Configure: UP 258 (PrphDev - subsystem-specific extension)
  • Switch on/off delay (BI - subsystem-specific extension)
  • Configure: UP 258 wide PIR (PrphDev - subsystem-specific extension)
  • Configure: UP 258 wide US (PrphDev - subsystem-specific extension)
