添加序列号

所有自动化站均附有可粘贴的地址标签，其中包含唯一的序列号和条形码。地址贴纸可以在安装过程中去除并粘贴到建筑物平面图上。

您可以使用条形码扫描仪或在调试后读回数据来添加序列号。

 

Add the serial number by using a barcode scanner

  1. A barcode scanner is connected to your computer.
  1. In Building > Building structure, click in the Serial number field of the device.
  2. Scan the barcode of the device.
  1. The serial number of the device is added to the Serial number field.

 

Add the serial number by data read back

  1. A device is configured and connected.
  1. Download automation stations.
    Configure and download
  2. Upload an automation station configuration.
  1. The serial number of the device is added to the Serial number field.
