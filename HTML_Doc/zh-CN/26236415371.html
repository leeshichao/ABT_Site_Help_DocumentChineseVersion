[TX2_DMD] tx2 需求

功能块 TX2_DMD 计算加热、冷却、加湿和除湿过程的加权需求信号，由功能块 TX2_ACTN 使用，以实现 ERC（能量回收）的成本优化使用。

TX2_DMD 计算加热、冷却、加湿和除湿过程的加权需求信号，功能块 TX2_ACTN 使用该信号对 ERC（能量回收）进行成本优化控制。当前需求由各个空气处理聚合的定位信号和聚合的设计值确定。需求根据能源消耗或成本进行加权。

Functioning

Control strategy Economizer TX2

节能器 TX2 控制策略包括两个重点：

  • ERC control with regard to energy-optimized air handling processes.
  • Control of air handling processing with regard to weighting (e.g. costs).

功能块 TX2_DMD 和 TX2_ACTN 是节能器 TX2 控制策略的一部分，具有以下功能：

  • The function block TX2_DMD calculates energy-optimized air handling processes (based on enthalpy and aggregates used) and then calculates an energy-optimized demand for ERC. The energy-optimized demand signals additionally are multiplied by a weighting factor (e.g. with regard to costs), so that several weighted (e.g. costly) air handling processes have a superordinate demand for ERC. ERC then focuses on weighted (i.e. costly) air handling processes. Cheaper air handling processes thus are carried out mostly by other air handling processes.
  • The function block TX2_ACTN controls ERC based on the demand signals weighted by function block TX2_DMD. Here, the function block TX2_ACTN determines the optimal operating point for ERC.

Process procedure in the function block

步

功能块中的处理过程

1

该功能块计算当前有效的能源使用量：

  • The positioning signals for air handling aggregates (e.g. cooler, humidifier, heater) are evaluated.
  • The design values for the aggregates are considered.

2

该功能块计算 ERC ([WgHDmd]、[WgCDmd]、[WgHuDmd]、[WgDhuDmd]) 的加权需求信号，以便将空气处理过程（例如冷却）的成本总和优化到最低：

  • The weighting of the individual air handling processes is considered.
  • A difference is made between steam humidifier and air washer.
  • The efficiency degree of ERC is considered.

Engineering

Plant configuration

Economizer tx2 控制策略用于以下工厂配置：

  • ERC, preheater, cooler, reheater, humidifier
  • ERC, cooler, reheater, humidifier

（ERG = 混合空气阻尼器、旋转式热交换器、板式热交换器等）

仅当存在至少三个空气处理过程且 ERC 允许湿度恢复时，才能发挥 Economizer tx2 策略的优势。再循环空气最适合湿度恢复。

Adiabatic cooling

如果加湿器用作绝热冷却的空气净化器，空气净化器必须放置在冷却盘管之后，以防止冷却盘管除湿。

Temperature sensor [TEx], [HuAbsEx]

如果ERC之前的排风与室内空气温差较大，则必须在ERC之前的排风中放置一个额外的温度传感器。

Weighting [WgH], [WgC], [WgHu], [WgDhu]

可以考虑以下成本因素来衡量各个空气处理过程：

  • Primary energy costs.
  • Operations and amortization costs for corresponding air handling.
  • Efficiency across entire process.
Application

功能块 TX2_DMD 和 TX2_ACTN 一起形成 ERC 控制。这些功能块与空调系统的房间和送风控制（至少 3 个空气处理过程）相关联，形成一种控制策略 (Economizer tx2)。

Energy efficiency

Economizer tx2 控制策略可用作其他能源和成本优化程序的补充：

  • Peak demand optimization
  • Ventilation time optimization: Scheduler program, Optimum Start Stop OSSC.
  • Setpoint field control (tolerance band for temperature and humidity)
  • Night ventilation
  • Return limitation for district heating
Process response

该功能块处理输入处的值。这些值可以是功能块的默认值，也可以是输出值或预配置功能块的输出值。
