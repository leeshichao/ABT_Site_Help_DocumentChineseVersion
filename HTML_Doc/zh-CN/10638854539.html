HVAC 工厂运行模式，用于热泵 (HvacPltMod17)

Overview

应用功能》HVAC plant operating mode 17, for fan coil unit, heat pump, radiant ceiling, radiator" (HvacPltMod17) 选择 HVAC 设备操作模式并考虑房间操作模式、存在检测、HVAC 存在模式和窗户接触。预热和冷却请求信号通过中央操作模式AF 接收。

Function

  • The Plant operating mode (PltOpMod) can have the following states:
    1:Off
    2:Protection
    3:Economy
    4:Pre-Comfort
    5:Comfort
    6:Warm-up
    7:Cool down
    8:Room low temperature protection
    9:Condensation overflow protection
    10:Free cooling
    11:Night cooling
    12:Ventilation
    13:Equipment temperature protection
    14:Air volume flow off
    15:Not used
    16:Not used
    17:Not used
  • The AF reads the room operating mode, presence detector, HVAC presence mode the window contact. Commands from central AFs are also received.
  • The AF outputs the plant operating mode (PltOpMod), providing it to the room segments via a BACnet object, internal connection, and grouping.
  • Switching delays can be configured to smooth plant mode switching (see Configuration).

 

Next room operating mode (NxROpMod)

为了提高能源效率，NxROpMod 可以设置为匹配预计在预热或冷却后的房间操作模式。一个典型的用例是很少使用的会议室，通常在热身或冷却后进入预舒适状态。将 NxROpMod 设置为 Pre-Comfort 将允许在预热或冷却阶段使用 Pre-Comfort 设置点而不是 Comfort 设置点，从而防止空间过冷或过热。
