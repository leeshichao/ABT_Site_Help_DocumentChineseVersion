将群组成员分配给群组管理员

InBuilding > Central functions，您可以在“详细信息”窗格中将群组成员分配给群组管理员（Group members).
Group member details

 

Assign a group member to a group manager

  1. A central function is created.
  1. Select a group manager.
  2. In the Details pane (Group member), the corresponding group members display.
  3. In the Assigned column, select the corresponding check boxes of the group members you want to assign.
  4. Click Assign selected.
  1. The selected group members are assigned to the group manager.
