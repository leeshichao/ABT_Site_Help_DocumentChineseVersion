SWI_DLY：开启和关闭延迟

块 SWI_DLY (FB887) 处理输入信号：

  • Delay switch-on or switch-off.
  • Cancel switch-on signal; cancel delay times.
  • A switch-on pulse signal or switch-off pulse signal is also generated.

发挥作用

延迟开启或关闭。

 

打开和关闭的最小脉冲长度。

 

取消开机信号。

 

取消延迟时间。

过程响应

  • If for Start [In] = 1 then [DlyOnTi] starts.
    After expiration of [DlyOnTi] switches to [Out] = 1 and [SwiOnPulse] is triggered.
  • For Start [In] = 0 then [Out] remains = 0, [DlyOffTi] is not started and [SwiOffPulse] is not triggered.
