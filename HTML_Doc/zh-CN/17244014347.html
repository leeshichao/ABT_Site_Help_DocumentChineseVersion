卸载

您可以使用 ABT 安装程序中的卸载选项卸载 ABT 站点和 ABT Pro。请完成以下步骤来卸载 ABT Pro 和 ABT Site：

  1. Extract the ABT setup package by using ABT_Installer.exe.
  1. In welcome ABT Installer’s welcome dialog box select the language of your choice and click Next.
    Note:     The language you select here is for ABT Installer only and not the ABT Pro or Site product language.
  2. Select Uninstall to uninstall ABT Site from your machine and click Next.
  4. Select the feature that you want to uninstall and click Next.
  5. Verify the features you have selected to uninstall and click Uninstall.
  6. Select Restart Now and click Restart to restart your machine.
  1. You have successfully uninstalled ABT Pro and ABT Site on your machine.
