Dimming actuators product description

Dimming actuators are KNX devices with one or more dimmer outputs. The bus is connected via a bus terminal block. The dimming actuators can switch and dim resistive loads (e.g. incandescent lamps, high voltage halogen lamps), capacitive loads (e.g. low voltage halogen lamps with intermediate electronic transformers), or inductive loads (e.g. low voltage halogen lamps with intermediate conventional transformers).
