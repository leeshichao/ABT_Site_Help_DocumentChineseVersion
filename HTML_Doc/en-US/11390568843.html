Odp_QMX3.P87_Fh

Wall-mounted fume hood operating display QMX3.P87

The Operator Display Panel (ODP) is the interface between the operator and the Fume Hood Controller (FHC).

  • LCD display for volume flow setpoint, face velocity setpoint and alarms
  • Alarm and warning notifications
  • Multiple operator modes
  • Scheduler override
  • "Green Leaf" energy efficiency function
  • Spare programmable buttons (6)
