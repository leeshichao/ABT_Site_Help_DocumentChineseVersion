VAV compact controller

VAV compact controllers are KNX devices with a differential pressure sensor (operating range 0..300 Pa), a damper actuator and an air volume control loop. They can be used for plants with variable or constant airflow. The angular rotation of the air damper is mechanically adjustable between 0° and 90°.
