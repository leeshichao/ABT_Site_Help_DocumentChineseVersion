[GET_DATI] Get local date and time of day

Function block GET_DATI provides the date and time of the automation station.

GET_DATI provides the date and time of the automation station. The system time is available on the outputs in various formats: Date and time, date, time of day. and is split into values for year, month, day, hour, minute, second, millisecond, day of week, and day of year.
