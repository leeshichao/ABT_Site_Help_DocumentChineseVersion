RXM21.1 product description

I/O blocks contain the inputs and outputs that are controlled by a room automation station via KNX PL-Link. They are compatible with PXC3.. series room automation stations.

RXM21.1, KNX PL-Link I/O block

  • 1 temperature input (LG-Ni 1000)
  • 2 binary inputs
  • 3 potential-free relay contacts for staged fans
  • 4 triac outputs (AC 24 V) for thermal valve actuators, motor driven valves and damper actuators

Order number: S55376-C104
