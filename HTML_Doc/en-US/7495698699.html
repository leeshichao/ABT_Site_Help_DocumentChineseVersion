Supply chain relief function (SplyRlfFnct11)

Overview

The application function "Supply chain relief function 11" (SplyRlfFnct11) calculates the demand on the AHU to make sure it is sufficient to ensure operational stability (control of the static pressure) of the primary AHU. If demand is too low, the relief command (RlfCmd) is distributed to the VAV controllers in the segment, which respond by increasing the air flow setpoint.

Function

If the overall demand for central air flow (number of air demands, NumAirDmd) is too low, the relief command (RlfCmd) is set to 1:On and distributed via grouping to the VAVs of the segment(s), to induce more air volume flow at the central AHU.

In the following illustration, the AHU is started at the minimum level of two demands, and relief (RlfCmd) is set to On and will remain on until/unless 10 demands are exceeded.
