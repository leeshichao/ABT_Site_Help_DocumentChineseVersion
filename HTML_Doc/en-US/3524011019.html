UP 220 device properties

The listed properties are displayed in the Hardware and network editor > Inspector window > Properties tab when the device with KNX PL-Link is selected in the Device view tab.

Light dimming sensor basic (LDSB)

Property

Description

Default value

Push button function

Push button function defines if 1 or 2 pushbuttons are used for switching or dimming and defines the behavior of the pushbuttons.

Switching & dimming: 1 pushbutton is used. A short key press toggles between switching the light on or off. A long key press toggles between dimming the lights up or down.

On & dimming up: 1 pushbutton is used. A short key press switches the light on and a long key press dims the light up.

Off & dimming down: 1 pushbutton is used. A short key press switches the light off and a long key press dims the light down.

2 buttons, switching & dimming: 2 pushbuttons are used. One of the buttons switches the light on or dims the light up the other switches the light off or dims the light down.

Switching & dimming

Polarity inverse

Polarity inverse defines how dimming up and dimming down are assigned to the buttons.

: Left or lower button: dimming up, right or upper button: dimming down.

: Left or lower button: dimming down, right or upper button: dimming up.

: Left or lower button: dimming up, right or upper button: dimming down.
