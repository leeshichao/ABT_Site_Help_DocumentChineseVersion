Failure level defines the level of light in case of a failure of the DALI communication.

Unit: none

Power-on level

Power-on level defines the level of light the device goes to after a power-return.

The device reacts to DALI commands 0.5 s after power-on. If no command affecting power level is received within 0.6 s after mains power-on, the device will go to Power-on level immediately without fading.

Range: 0…255

0: light is off.

1: light is on.
