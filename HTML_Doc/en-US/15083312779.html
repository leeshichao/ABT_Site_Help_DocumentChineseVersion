[DATI] Date and time of day functions

Function block DATI adds a time period to a point in time on a specific day and/or subtracts a time period from the point in time on a specific day.

DATI adds or subtracts a time period to/from a point in time on a specific day. A time period can be added while simultaneously subtracting a time period. The result is provided on output [Out], split into values for date, time, year, month, day, day of week, day of year, hour, minute, second, and millisecond.
