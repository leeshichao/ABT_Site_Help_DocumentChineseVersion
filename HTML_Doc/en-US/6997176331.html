Central solar radiation from weather station (CenSolWths11)

Overview

Application function "Central solar radiation from weather station 11" (CenSolWths11) calculates solar radiation against the facade based on a central global radiation sensor on a weather station.

Function

A sensor on a weather station measures global radiation.

The AF calculates global radiation against the facade.

Note: AF CenGlrPrt11 provides the calculated effective solar radiation on the facade in SolRdnEff.
