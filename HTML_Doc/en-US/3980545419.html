MUX8_4V: Multiplexer 1 of 8 values, 4 val

The MUX8_4V (FC444) blocks switches one of the 8 multistate inputs to the outputs in dependence of a selector. The multiple inputs have two multistate values and two real values available.

Functionality

The block switches inputs [In0xx]-[In7xx] to outputs [Outxx] defined by selector [K].

If the value of selector [K] is smaller than 0 or greater than 7, the output [Outxx] is set to zero.
