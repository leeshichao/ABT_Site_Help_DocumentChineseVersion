Analog output PWM with constant pulse period

  • For triac modules
  • 0...100 [%]
  • Typically for electromechanic actuators

AO_AO_Pulsewidth modulation: Thermal_PWM_0-100

Analog output PWM with variable pulse period

  • For triac modules
  • 0...100 [%]
  • Typically for thermal actuators, without undercool- and overheat functionality

AO_AO_Pulsewidth modulation: Thermal_PWM_5-50

Analog output PWM with variable pulse period

  • For triac modules
  • 5...50 [%]
  • Typically for thermal actuators, with undercool- and overheat functionality
    • 5 [%] = actuator pre-heated but fully closed
    • 50 [%] = actuator fully open
