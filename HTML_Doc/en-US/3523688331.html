KNX PL-Link devices

Devices with KNX Peripheral Link (devices with KNX PL-Link) and devices with KNX S-Mode communicate via KNX PL-Link bus. These devices with KNX PL-Link usually work as plug & play devices.

The KNX PL-Link bus only supports SI units. Therefore properties need to be configured in SI units.

For predefined devices in the library ready for use, see Library.
