Central functions

The Central functions task allows you to manage group managers and assign group members of a building. Grouping hierarchies can be created, modified and deleted. The group managers can be assigned to a hierarchy element and the group members can be assigned to their actual groups.
Grouping

Assignment of group members and group managers in ABT Pro projects are only possible within ABT Pro, they are read-only in the Building component. The information managed is saved in the common part of a customer project.

Checks the consistency of the created central function hierarchy. The building structure is compared to the central function hierarchy. If duplicates are found, a warning is logged.

Create cache

Updates the list of group managers from automation stations created in different ABT Pro projects. The function is only used in connection with BOS (Branch Office Server).
