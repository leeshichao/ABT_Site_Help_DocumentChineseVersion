Central optimum start/stop control (CenOssc11)

Overview

The application function "Central optimum start stop control 11, Desigo V6.0 (primary)" (CenOssc11) distributes the start/stop information from the optimizer to subordinate AFs CenOssc11 or the connected rooms. In addition, it reads the room temperature from the connected rooms and provides the average, the average of the 10 lowest and the average of the 10 highest values to superordinate AFs and to BACnet objects.
