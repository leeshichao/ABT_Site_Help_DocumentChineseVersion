Central functions

Overview

The application type "Type central functions11" (CenFnct11) is a maximum software solution for superposed room automation functions.

The type includes commonly used AFs to centrally operate electrical and mechanical installations HVAC, lighting, and shading.

There may be multiple instances for certain AFs, e.g. central facade functions for the 4 cardinal points.

Distribution of information

Information from the following functions is distributed via grouping to the connected rooms.

  • Central weather information
  • Central operation
  • Central service functions
  • Central protection and emergency functions
  • Functions for HVAC supply chains

Distribution can be organized hierarchically.
Hierarchical grouping

Collect and distribute information in supply chains

Information from the connected rooms are collected or distributed via grouping:

  • Demand messages for supply chains (chilled water, hot water, air)
  • State messages from supply chains

Collection and distribution can be organized (bundled) hierarchically.
Bundling supply AFs

Group categories

The following group categories are available:

  • Occupancy
  • Lighting
  • Shading
  • HVAC
  • Facade
  • Weather station
  • Supply

They can be filtered on the management station.

Sub-categories

Depending on the function, communication is at a high priority to prevent unwanted local operation.
Priorities

Function

Priority

Emergency function

Emg = Emergency, for example, fire or evacuation, the light is switched on and the blinds are opened.

Service function

Srv = Service, for example, moving blinds to a defined position to clean windows.

Protection function

Prt = Protection, for example, to close blinds at low outside temperatures or high solar radiation.

Operation

Op = Operation, by scheduler, operator intervention via BACnet client or local operator unit.

Central commands can originate from the following sources:

  • External system or third-party device
  • System user via BACnet client
  • Building user via BACnet client or local operating element
  • Scheduler or reaction program
  • Superposed location based on grouping function.

Supply chains primary plants and room

Demand and forced signals from various consumers can be collected, evaluated and forwarded to the following components via grouping:

  • SplyAir: To the primary air handling units
  • SplyHw: To heating circuits
  • SplyChw: To cooling circuits

Demand-controlled operation of primary plants is an important element for efficient energy use.

The supply changes are also used to protect plants, aggregates, or components (e.g. the air handling unit may not force air against closed dampers or VAV boxes).
