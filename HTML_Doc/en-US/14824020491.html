[BIT_I] Conversion binary to integer value

Function block BIT_I summarizes 8 individual boolean inputs to one single integer output.

BIT_I summarizes 8 individual boolean inputs to one single integer output.

Functioning

BIT_I summarizes 8 inputs [In0]...[In7] as one output [Out]. At the same time, [In0] is mapped to bit 1 of output [Out], [In1] to bit 2 of output [Out], etc.

Application

BIT_I allows for summarizing individual information as one line, to be resolved into individual information via I_BIT. BIT_I and I_BIT are intended, e.g., to prep values for communication, primarily with third-party components.
