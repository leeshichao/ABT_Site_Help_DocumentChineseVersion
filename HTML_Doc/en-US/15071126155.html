Showing and hiding pins

The green triangles at the bottom of a block indicate that some pins are hidden. You can select which pins are visible.

Show and hide pins

  1. In the Programming editor, select an element.
  2. Open the Properties tab.
  3. Open the Properties tab of the selected element.
  4. Click Interface and select or clear the check boxes in the Visible column, for example, PrVal and Dstb.

 

Multi-selection of pins

  1. Select the status or .
  2. Select the blue point of the rectangular .
  3. Drag the blue point up or down to the desired destination.
  1. All selected check boxes are active or cleared.
