Fan coil supply temperature cascade control heating (FcuTCasCtlH11)

Overview

The application function "Fan coil unit temperature cascade control for heating 11" (FcuTCasCtlH11) is the hub for the sequencing of the room temperature controllers for FCU heating. It determines the room heating setpoint and the room heating demand signals to maintain the room temperature at the heating setpoint.

For the heating coils, the room demand output signal is designed for cascaded supply temperature control in the room segment. (air sensor(s) required)

The following devices in the room segment(s) are supported:

  • Heating/Cooling coil
  • Heating/Chilled ceiling
  • Heating coil
  • Floor heating
  • Radiator
  • Fan

Function

This AF works in collaboration with a room segment HVAC device component heating AF. The heating room temperature PID controller is enabled based on a signal that is read via the grouping function (from equipment in the room segment) indicating whether heating is available.

Heating devices are activated in the following order (default):
Floor heating > Radiator > Heated ceiling > Heating coil > Fan

Engineering and commissioning

Radiant floor is likely to change room temperature slower than other HVAC equipment. For specific projects, it may be useful to slow down the action of the relevant room temperature controller (continuous, PID) or change the controller mode to 2-position.
