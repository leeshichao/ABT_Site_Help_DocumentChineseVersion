[SWICNV_B] Switch converter to Boolean (EnSwi und ValSwi)

Function block SWICNV_B is used to connect standardized manual switches.

SWICNV_B is used to connect standardized manual switches.

Functioning

SWICNV_B converts a binary or multistate input signal of a manual switch to two binary outputs.
