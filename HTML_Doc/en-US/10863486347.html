Configure: UP 220 (PrphDev - subsystem-specific extension)

The Peripheral Device (PrphDev) object supports following properties of the subsystem-specific extension Configure: UP220.

✳ Indicates a Siemens proprietary property.

Swap button pair 1 (AB) ✳

 

 

"Swap button pair 1 (AB)" defines how the dimming or moving direction is assigned to the buttons A and B.

No

The logic of pushbutton pairs for on/off and up/down is not inverted.

Yes

The logic of pushbutton pairs for on/off and up/down is inverted.

Swap button pair 2 (CD) ✳

 

 

"Swap button pair 2 (CD)" defines how the dimming or moving direction is assigned to the buttons C and D.

No

The logic of pushbutton pairs for on/off and up/down is not inverted.

Yes

The logic of pushbutton pairs for on/off and up/down is inverted.
