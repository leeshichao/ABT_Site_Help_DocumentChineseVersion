Downloading an updated certificate to a device

This procedure describes how to update the operational or root certificate in the selected device for:

  • Web
    Renewing web internal operational certificate
  • BACnet/SC
    Renewing BACnet/SC internal operational certificate
    BACnet/SC certification management in the projects
  • IEEE802.1X
    Renewing the IEEE 802.1X internal operational certificate

 

Download a certificate to a device

  1. A device is connected and discovered.
  2. A full download with an operational certificate is already performed.
  1. Open the Startup > Configure and download task.
  2. In the Engineered devices tab, right-click a device that matches a discovered device.
  3. Select Load and than Only updated certificates.
  1. You have downloaded and updated an operational or root certificate to a device.
