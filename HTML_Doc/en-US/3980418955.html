CMP_MS: Comparison of multistate

The CMP_MS (FB462) block compares two multistate values and supplies the logical result (Yes, No).

Functionality

The block compares the two input values [In1] and [In2] and sets either 1 (Yes) or 0 (No) at the beginning of the output in dependence of the result [Eq].
